A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions. Clear answers.

Still exploring? Let’s talk through your workflow.

Is 7Network only a WhatsApp system?+

7Network connects CRM, sales pipeline, customer conversations, campaigns, automation and team tasks. Business categories add relevant workflows, such as courses, enrolment and collections for training centers. WhatsApp is one part of the connected workflow.

Can it fit my industry?+

We design your CRM around your business process, with category-based views, configurable features and workflows. Training Center is one implementation. Tell us your industry and requirements so we can agree the setup, integrations and any bespoke development needed.

How does my team work together?+

Use shared customer context, lead ownership, follow-up tasks and role-based access. Available roles and seat limits depend on the business category and assigned plan.

What can we automate?+

Use visual flows and guided chatbots for common questions, enquiry collection, routing and handoff. Channel availability, AI configuration and provider requirements are reviewed during setup.

Are finance and courses included for every business?+

Features are category and plan dependent. Training Center workflows support courses, learners, enrolment, invoices and collections. We’ll confirm the finance workflows and limits available for your business before setup.

How do I get started?+

Send a demo request below with your business and priorities. Your enquiry is delivered to the 7Network team for review and follow-up. We’ll discuss the right configuration and current terms with you.
