Team extensions

Assign SaleMaX extension labels to active owners, managers and agents. These assignments are not created in Asterisk; each matching PBX endpoint must be provisioned before calling can be enabled.

Use 3–8 digits. Leave the field blank and save to clear an assignment.

Call queues

Configure tenant-owned queue policy and members with assigned extensions. Saving this policy does not create an Asterisk queue or enable calls; the platform PBX and gateway routes must still be provisioned.
