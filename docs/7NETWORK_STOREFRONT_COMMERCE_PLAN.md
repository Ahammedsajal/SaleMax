# 7Network Storefront Commerce: implementation and rollout plan

**Status: deployed behind entitlements, 6 November 2026.** EC01 private Products and Inventory is live. EC02 storefront and guest checkout plus the first EC03 dispatch, cash-receipt, partial-return and reconciliation workflows are deployed as an app-only overlay. Production has no entitled plan assignment and no enabled store, so a separately approved pilot is still required. This plan extends the retail operations portion of the [7Network roadmap](7NETWORK_VISION_ROADMAP.md); it does not close or narrow the existing 42-ticket upgrade or its release gates.

## 1. Product decision

**Products and Inventory are private business tools.** Eligible businesses use them in the signed-in `/user` panel to manage their catalog and stock, including when they sell in person, by phone or through another channel. The public storefront is an optional feature with its own setup, permissions and publication. Saving a product keeps its record and images private. A business that enables its storefront explicitly selects which products and images to publish from Storefront settings.

Give a business with no online shop **one branded link it can share, a simple catalog editor and one place to fulfil orders**. Begin in Qatar with a few merchants selling fixed-price, packaged goods, one stock and dispatch location per store, QAR, Arabic and English, local delivery and pickup, cash on delivery (COD) and pay at pickup. Choose a business that controls its own stock and drivers. A gift shop, boutique or accessories seller is a better first pilot than a supermarket with weighed items or a restaurant with kitchen, modifier and allergen rules.

Reuse `retail_boutique` and existing category, entitlement and plan tooling where the fit is verified. “Store” is the merchant-facing workspace label and a capability a qualifying category can offer. “Gift boxes” and “Dates” are merchant-owned catalog categories. These are separate concepts. Enable later categories by reviewed capability contracts, rather than assigning every business to retail or duplicating the catalog for each category.

| Direction | Best use | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Curated storefront](assets/storefront-commerce/01-curated-storefront.png) | Small merchants with a focused collection; easy to understand from a shared mobile link | **Pilot default.** Home leads into a searchable shop with the full order journey. |
| [2. Catalog first](assets/storefront-commerce/02-catalog-first.png) | Wider ranges and repeat shoppers who know what they want | Later setting for the same template, products and checkout. Prioritize search, filtering and reorder over a hero. |
| [3. Shop and quote](assets/storefront-commerce/03-shop-and-quote.png) | Corporate gifts, wholesale or custom items where price or availability needs review | Next sales extension. Use the same storefront; add a quote CTA that opens a tenant lead with product context. Ready-stock products keep their cart flow. |

**Link:** reuse the existing hosted pattern `https://7network.live/business/<tenant-slug>`. Publish from the existing business panel; show Live/Draft/Paused, Open store, Copy link, Share and a QR that contains only the public URL. The hosted link should work without DNS setup. A verified custom domain is an optional alias or canonical origin after hostname, TLS, cookie and two-tenant checks. Keep the stable hosted link usable; do not promise to transfer a live cart between domains. Examples and mockups are fictional.

**Commercial model:** start with a storefront subscription or add-on managed through the existing plans, with transparent product, storage and driver seat limits. Offer custom domains and advanced reporting on a higher tier once verified. Delay commissions and platform-held funds. A useful acquisition loop: give each merchant a shareable QR for their Instagram bio, WhatsApp catalog and counter; report orders and qualified enquiries by channel using opaque campaign codes and aggregated metrics.

## 3. Seller workflow and operations screens

In the existing business `/user` panel, show **Products** and **Inventory** as direct entries under **Business Operations** for eligible members. Products opens the maintained Products & Services editor. Inventory opens its own stock screen. Each product links to its stock, and each stock row links back to the product. Gate both screens by business capability and staff permission, independently of storefront enablement, domains and publication. Enforce the same rules on direct URLs and API calls. Give the optional public sales workflow a separate **Storefront settings** entry.

**Add a product to the business catalog.** Open Products → **Add product**. Take or select a photo, enter a name and selling price, pick or create a category inline, and enter opening stock. Choose **Save product** to make it available to authorized staff. For a stocked product, create its default SKU and opening balance in one transaction. Use Inventory for later stock changes. Support services in the shared editor without requiring a stock record. At pilot, show translated labels and a clear fallback for missing merchant-entered content.

Keep description, extra photos, SKU, variants and sort order under **More options**. Variants have independent price, SKU and stock when enabled. Size images server-side, remove EXIF, show upload progress and retry, and keep originals private. Add preview-first CSV import after the single-product flow works.

| Existing-panel view | Main job |
| --- | --- |
| Products | Add or edit photos, name, price, SKU or variants, and category in the private catalog. Filter active, archived and out-of-stock items. Show available quantity with an **Open inventory** shortcut for stocked products. Preserve archived product references in transaction history. |
| Inventory | Search by product or SKU and filter low or out-of-stock items. Show **On hand**, **Reserved**, and **Available** by SKU. The pilot defaults to one location. Authorized staff can **Receive stock**, **Adjust count** with a reason, and view **Movement history** with actor, time, quantities and linked order where applicable. Add a configurable low-stock threshold and a direct link to the product editor. |
| Storefront settings, optional | Configure the brand, select products and images from the business catalog, set delivery or pickup fees, review policies, preview and publish. Show **Copy link** once live. Require separate publishing permission and use an explicit public projection. |
| Retail Orders | Order queue with filters, order detail, items/price/fee snapshots, preparation and pickup, exceptions, audit timeline, cancellation/return reason and linked customer. Owner or authorized staff can record a phone/WhatsApp order through the same checkout invariants in a later increment. |
| Dispatch | Ready/unassigned queue, scoped driver availability, manual assign/reassign, handoff and failed-attempt queue. A pickup order never enters the driver queue. |
| My Deliveries | Driver signs in with an individual invited business membership using the current sign-in experience and lands on a narrow phone-friendly route in the existing shell. List assigned jobs; accept or report a problem; see pickup and dropoff, allowed customer contact, package summary, amount to collect and instructions. Provide **Google Maps**, **Waze**, **Picked up**, **Delivered**, and **Report issue** as applicable. |
| Finance / Reports | COD due, collected by driver, handed over, verified and discrepancies; accountant reconciles each settlement once. Show sales, returns and collection totals separately. Keep bookkeeping tied to the approved finance contract. |

The owner mockup predates the private catalog decision. Use **Save product** in Products and put **Publish** and **Copy storefront link** in Storefront settings. Show “Start delivery” before `out_for_delivery`, and show “Delivered” and “Report issue” once the driver is out. Use maintained 7Network brand assets and grant drivers access only to their assigned delivery workflow. [Owner, dispatch and driver visual board](assets/storefront-commerce/store-operations.png).

Inventory and checkout use the same SKU balance. If 12 units are on hand and 3 are reserved by open orders, the store can sell 9. Receiving adds to on hand. Cancelling an unfulfilled order releases its reservation. Handoff consumes stock and clears the reservation together. Block count adjustments below reserved quantity and surface the affected orders for resolution. Save stock movements and balance updates together with retry protection, and use a revision check for absolute count adjustments so a stale screen cannot overwrite a recent receipt or sale. Give stock read and stock adjustment separate permissions, independent of product editing and dispatch.

## 4. Public storefront screens

Keep merchant branding prominent. Use one accessible header on all pages: Home, Shop/Menu, About, Contact, language switch and Cart. Footer includes delivery/returns, privacy and merchant support. Merchant theme comes from safe semantic tokens and approved color contrasts; no tenant HTML/JavaScript injection.

| Order confirmation / tracking | Clear receipt reference and protected order link with status, expected next step and support. Show compact timeline; no internal notes or driver GPS. Expiring, revocable high-entropy guest access, with reviewed recovery/renewal, rather than lookup by order number or phone alone. | Contact | Public phone/WhatsApp when configured, hours, an accessible enquiry form and order support entry. Use existing enquiry and Lead Pipeline adapters with abuse controls and no customer details in the link. An order support enquiry may reference an order only after a scoped verification. |

## 5. Commerce and delivery rules

1. **Tenant and stock.** Resolve a public tenant from a validated hosted slug or verified host, then check active tenant, storefront publication, commerce and checkout flags, eligible branch and plan. A client-provided tenant/branch/price is never authority. Derive the branch from the selected approved pickup option or delivery zone. For checkout, lock SKU stock rows in deterministic order; validate current prices, stock and fee, create immutable order and line snapshots, reserve inventory, audit and enqueue outbox work together. Check quantity caps and reservation abuse. Store QAR in integer minor units. Same idempotency key with the same payload returns the original result; a different payload conflicts. Configure bounded reservations, cancellation and expiry rules; reconcile held stock, and move picked-up inventory to a dispatched/consumed state exactly once. A returned item is restocked only after its physical condition is accepted.
2. **Order, delivery, payment.** Keep these as separate state machines. Map compatible order states (`confirmed → preparing → ready → completed`, with `out_for_delivery`, cancellation and return exceptions) rather than changing status meanings silently. Delivery: `unassigned → assigned → accepted → picked_up → delivered`, with reassign/retry, failed attempt and return-to-store handling. Payment: due, collection reported, settlement verified, disputed/reversed/refunded as appropriate. Record driver, revision, actor, time, handoff and reason for each transition. Driver may report cash collected or an exception; Finance verifies settlement. A delivered status does not settle payment; an unpaid COD handoff needs an explicit exception path. Stale versions and double assignment fail safely. Choose a minimal handoff proof during pilot, e.g. a one-time code, with a logged exception path; no mandatory customer or door photography.
3. **Navigation and privacy.** Store a validated destination pin and textual address; build [Google Maps Directions URLs](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started#directions-action) and [Waze deep links](https://developers.google.com/waze/deeplinks/) from coordinates, with a copy-address fallback. Use `https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=<encoded-lat,lng>&travelmode=driving` and `https://waze.com/ul?ll=<encoded-lat,lng>&navigate=yes` as integration patterns. Send only coordinates to map providers. Show address/contact only while the authenticated driver is assigned to the active job; revoked/reassigned users lose access immediately, including deep links and real-time subscriptions. Keep completed driver history minimal and exclude raw address/phone from routine logs, exports and alerts. No background GPS in the pilot. Handle offline as unconfirmed until the server acknowledges an action.
4. **Leads and customer data.** An enquiry or quote request creates or links a private, tenant-scoped contact and a Pipeline opportunity with store/source/product context, owner and next task. A successful order creates an order and links the tenant contact and activity; avoid creating a second open opportunity for every purchase. Normalize and deduplicate within each tenant while handling shared numbers and ambiguous matches. Don't create a lead for an anonymous abandoned cart. Any follow-up needs the channel-specific contact rights and consent required by the existing system. Keep service notices, opt-in marketing and future network sharing separate. A later customer requirement/proposal can link to a completed order with explicit customer visibility and contact grants.
5. **Payments and returns.** Pilot: merchant COD or pay at pickup, cancellation and return intake, handling notes and cash reconciliation. Extend validated finance primitives after commerce posting rules, merchant invoice/returns policy and retention are reviewed; do not reuse training-specific invoice assumptions. Later: merchant-owned hosted payment checkout with signature checks, deduplication, out-of-order events, refunds, webhook reconciliation and recovery. Never infer a paid state from the browser return. No card storage, pooled funds, automated payouts or escrow in this scope.
6. **Notifications and recovery.** Keep the tracking page authoritative. Send order received, ready, dispatched, delivered and failure notices via the existing durable outbox and only for a configured channel and permitted recipient. Workers recheck status, recipient, assignment and permissions before sending; use bounded retries and deduplication. Keep internal alerts free of PII, and expose a support queue for stuck orders and driver/cash exceptions. Fulfilment survives a notification outage. External channels are a separate enablement gate.

Agree performance, support response and rollout targets with the pilot team at EC00. With release gates met, the next high-value extension is **“Send product” and “Turn enquiry into order” in the existing Inbox**, using current published price and stock and preserving the original lead/channel attribution.
